Hangout Launcher

Paste the newest Hangout link into the bar above and press Run.
How it works
  1. When the link changes, Website Maker sends you the new one in chat.
  2. Paste it in the bar and press Run. Hangout opens right here.
  3. Your last links are remembered on this device.
